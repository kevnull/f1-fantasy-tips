Race arc
Singapore is a pivotal weekend with 6 races remaining in the season. Russell's engine penalty here likely means another penalty later, creating a second opportunity for overtake-point harvesting — but possibly at a better overtaking venue. The split aero packages at Mercedes will reveal which specification is genuinely faster, informing constructor strategy through the final stretch. Red Bull's recent upward trajectory (strong in Malaysia and now sprint pole in Singapore) suggests Verstappen may be the reliable captain anchor through the season's end. Budget builders like Lindblad, Hadjar, and Hulkenberg remain essential holds as price rises compound heading into the final rounds. Teams chasing in leagues should look for differential plays in the rain-affected sessions rather than burning chips in uncertain conditions.
