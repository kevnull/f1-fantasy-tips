Race arc
This Bahrain GP in Malaysia is a pivotal budget-building week thanks to engine penalties creating cheap position-gain opportunities via Hadjar and Lindblad. The Mercedes upgrade makes triple-Mercedes (Antonelli + Russell + constructor) the high-floor play, but committing to that means potentially missing the overtake bonanza from penalized Red Bull/Racing Bulls drivers. Next week is the Singapore Sprint, which compounds scoring opportunities and is already showing rain in the forecast — making it the ideal No Negative chip deployment. Players should use this week to build budget through Lindblad and Hadjar price rises while positioning their rosters for the sprint weekend pivot, where 4A lineups with sprint-strong drivers will be critical.
