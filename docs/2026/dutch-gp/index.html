Race arc
The Dutch GP is the pivotal race bridging the first and second halves of the 2026 season. As a sprint weekend, it offers elevated scoring potential and is the ideal moment to deploy No Negative or 3x chips. The 4A lineup meta is crystallizing around Antonelli + one Ferrari driver + Ferrari/Mercedes constructors, and players within 2-3M of the ~125M budget threshold should prioritize price-gaining B-tier assets this week to unlock that configuration. Aston Martin's trajectory is the biggest storyline — their chassis upgrade in Hungary plus the expected Honda engine upgrade at Zandvoort could permanently reshape the B-tier landscape, making Alonso and Stroll premium budget enablers. After Zandvoort, Hadjar becomes much harder to hold as his rolling average normalizes, so the summer break is the time to plan the transition from a Hadjar-anchored midfield team to a full 4A lineup. Monza follows two weeks later with a very different track profile (low downforce, high overtakes), so transfers made now should also consider Italian GP viability.
