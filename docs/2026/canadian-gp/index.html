Chip strategy
WildcardWait
Use if you need 4+ transfers to get onto the Mercedes/McLaren meta and set up for Monaco. ReinFantasy is playing it due to needing to move off Hulkenberg, Lawson, Leclerc, and restructure constructors. FormulaFantasyHub says use it if you're not on any Mercedes assets and need wholesale changes, but a -10 hit (2 extra transfers) is fine if you only need 1-2 fixes. Don't use wildcard if you can fix your team with 3 or fewer transfers.
No NegativeSave
Rain risk has decreased significantly for Sunday. Montreal is historically a high-DNF track (3 per race over last 4 seasons), so keep it in reserve. Cannot use alongside Wildcard. ReinFantasy successfully used it in Miami saving 65 points.
LimitlessSave
Strong consensus to save Limitless for Monaco where the premium-driver advantage is enormous (100+ point delta vs non-limitless teams last year). Some managers used it in Miami successfully but most channels recommend Monaco.

Race arc
Canada is a pivotal setup race for Monaco, which follows shortly after. Monaco is historically brutal for midfield fantasy scorers (near-zero points for budget drivers) and is the prime weekend for the Limitless chip. The drivers you buy this weekend need to not only score well in Montreal's sprint format but also hold their price through Monaco — this eliminates volatile assets like Lawson and Bearman. The Mercedes upgrade debut and McLaren's continued development will clarify the constructor hierarchy heading into Monaco and the summer run of races. After Monaco, back-to-back races begin in earnest, so having a clean team with 2-3 transfers banked for the summer stretch is the strategic goal.
