Race arc
Azerbaijan kicks off the most grueling stretch of the season: three consecutive triple-headers with only one-week breaks between them (Baku→Malaysia→Singapore, then USA→Mexico→Brazil, then Vegas→Qatar→Abu Dhabi). Budget management is now paramount — every transfer counts double. Escaping Aston Martin and Cadillac price-floor assets is the strategic imperative entering this run. The Leclerc penalty saga will either resolve here or push to Malaysia/Singapore. Williams' upgrade verdict at Baku determines whether Albon becomes a season-long hold or a one-week rental. Lawson's seat situation should clarify by Malaysia. Players who cleanly transition to a 4A or 4.5A-light lineup by Singapore will have a significant structural advantage for the final 8 races. Save high-value chips (Limitless, 3x if available) for the sprint weekends in Singapore or later — the grind phase rewards consistency over boom-bust chip plays.
