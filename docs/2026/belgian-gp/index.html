Chip strategy
AutopilotWait
Multiple channels (FanAmp, F1FantasyPolePosition, FormulaFantasyHub) flag this as a strong Autopilot week. With Hadjar starting P21/P22 and potentially challenging Antonelli's score, Autopilot lets you hedge between Antonelli 2x and Hadjar 2x. FormulaFantasyHub explicitly titles their video 'USE AUTOPILOT THIS WEEK.' Best used if you have both Antonelli and Hadjar in your squad.
Final FixSave
FanAmp ran detailed analysis on using Final Fix to swap in Norris on Sunday — concluded it's not worth it. Norris's ceiling with a 10-place penalty (~30 pts best case) doesn't exceed a typical Antonelli Sunday (~35 pts). Save for a true emergency. Note: using Autopilot locks you out of Final Fix for the weekend.
No NegativeWait
FanAmp notes No Negative is 'always in play at Spa' due to the track's history of incidents and DNFs. However, DNFs have calmed down this season according to ZachGP. Consider if you're running risky assets or reliability-prone cars.
WildcardSave
No channel recommends using Wildcard this week. The meta transition to 4A lineups is not yet optimal — F1 Fantasy Tools sims and multiple channels suggest holding off one more week until Hungary when Aston Martin bring upgrades and the picture is clearer.

Race arc
Spa is the final race before Hungary, which is shaping up as a major inflection point. Aston Martin are bringing upgrades to the Hungaroring (where they were 3rd-best team in 2025), potentially making Alonso/Stroll viable again. Multiple channels note that the transition to 4A lineups (4 premium drivers) is approaching but not yet optimal — F1 Fantasy Tools sims suggest holding off one more week. Hungary is also a track where Verstappen historically excels, and the overtaking dynamics with new 2026 regs are unknown, making it a potential shake-up. For now, the strategy is to maximize budget building at Spa through Hadjar's penalty-fueled scoring and cheap B-tier price rises (Lindblad, Bortoleto, Colapinto all needing ~1 point for max rises), then reassess the meta for Hungary with upgraded Aston Martin data and clearer constructor pecking order.
