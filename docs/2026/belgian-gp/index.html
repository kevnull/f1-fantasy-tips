Chip strategy
AutopilotWait
Multiple channels highlight this as the best Autopilot weekend of the season. Hajar starting P21/P22 could outscore Antonelli on race day, making the chip valuable if you believe Hajar's race points exceed Antonelli's total. FormulaFantasyHub and F1FantasyPolePosition both seriously considering it. ZachGP notes Hajar is a viable Autopilot play. Use only if you genuinely believe Hajar will be the weekend's top scorer.
Final FixSave
Strong consensus to save. FanAmp ran detailed numbers: Norris's best-case Sunday (~30 pts) doesn't beat a typical Antonelli Sunday (~35 pts). ZachGP acknowledges it's a 'nice option' but recommends holding. F1FantasyPolePosition calls Final Fix a 'break glass in case of emergency' chip worth preserving.
No NegativeWait
FanAmp notes No Negative is 'always in play at Spa' due to the circuit's history of incidents and DNFs. Worth considering if you have high-risk assets, but not explicitly recommended over Autopilot this week.
WildcardSave
No channel recommends using Wildcard this week. Hungary with Aston Martin upgrades may be a better deployment.

Race arc
Belgium sets up a critical transition into Hungary. Aston Martin are bringing upgrades to the Hungaroring, potentially making Alonso and Stroll viable budget assets for the first time. Teams should prioritize budget building this week through Lindblad, Colapinto, and Bortoleto price rises while capturing Hajar's massive one-off scoring opportunity. The Red Bull constructor case strengthens if Verstappen maintains front-row form and Hajar delivers from the back. Hungary is a tighter, Monaco-style circuit where overtaking is harder, so the grid-penalty bonanza at Spa is a unique opportunity — capture the points now and pivot to a more qualifying-focused strategy for the Hungaroring. Hold Final Fix and Wildcard chips for future deployment when the value equation is clearer.
