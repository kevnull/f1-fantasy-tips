Race arc
Brazil is the penultimate or near-final opportunity to make significant team restructuring moves before the season-ending flyaway races. Clearing inactive drivers (Ocon, Hirakawa) is now critical as the -25 penalty per race compounds. The wet weather at Interlagos creates variance that could shake up the standings — a big Verstappen captain score here could lock in ranking gains heading into Las Vegas, where ML already projects Antonelli and Verstappen as the top two assets. Budget builders who lock in rising assets like Bortoleto and Sainz now will have more flexibility for Las Vegas and the finale. If you're holding Wildcard, the window to use it is narrowing rapidly — Brazil or Las Vegas are likely your last high-impact opportunities.
