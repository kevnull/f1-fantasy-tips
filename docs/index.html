Race arc
This Bahrain GP at Sepang is a pivotal budget-building race before the Singapore sprint weekend (Round 19). The engine penalties for Hadjar and Lindblad create rare guaranteed position-gain opportunities that should be exploited now. Mercedes' massive upgrade package could cement them as the dominant constructor through the end-of-season triple-header. Singapore next week is a sprint weekend with rain also forecast, making it the prime candidate for chip deployment (No Negative, Limitless, or 3x Captain). Players should use this week to lock in budget gains from penalty drivers and position their teams for the higher-variance sprint format. The ML data for Singapore already shows Mercedes (exp 66.8 constructor pts) and Antonelli (exp 26.2 pts) as clear frontrunners, suggesting continuity in the Mercedes core heading forward. Austin follows after Singapore, where Bearman historically performs well — making him a smart long-term Tier B hold.
