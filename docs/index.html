Race arc
The Spanish GP at the Madring is the highest-variance weekend remaining in the season, making it the ideal No Negative deployment. With most top players having already used their 3x chip at Monza, the grind phase of the season begins — rank movement will come from smart weekly decisions rather than chip-powered swings. Looking ahead, Baku follows after a short break: George Russell is expected to take an engine penalty there (creating a potential Antonelli 3x play for those who still have it), Hadjar should return for Red Bull (removing Lawson), and Williams is bringing a major upgrade package that could elevate Albon's value significantly. Budget building remains critical — Perez at the price floor, Hulkenberg as a reliable hold, and monitoring Lindblad/Borleto for min price rises are the moves that will pay dividends in the Middle Eastern swing. The key strategic question is whether to hold Ferrari constructor through Baku or pivot to McLaren, whose shorter gear ratios may suit the upcoming street circuits better.
