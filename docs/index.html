Race arc
Singapore is the final race of a brutal triple header, and the Russell engine saga will have ripple effects for the next 2-3 races. If Mercedes takes the penalty now, Russell loses his slot for the upcoming ADUO2 engine upgrade — a significant medium-term handicap. The next races should see that new Mercedes power unit arrive, potentially reshaping the 2x captain meta entirely. Red Bull's Baku upgrades appear to have durably improved Verstappen's competitiveness, and if he delivers again at Singapore, the captain conversation shifts firmly from Antonelli to Verstappen for the season's closing stretch. Budget builders acquired now (Lawson, Hadjar, Lindblad) will fund the premium 4A lineups needed when the calendar returns to power circuits where constructor choice becomes even more critical.
