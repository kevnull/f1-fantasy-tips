Race arc
Baku is the first of a late-season stretch where budget building becomes critical as most top players have exhausted their premium chips. The season is entering the 'grind phase' where consistent point accumulation and budget management matter more than chip-driven rank swings. Williams' rumored Baku upgrade could reshape the B-tier landscape for the remainder of the season. After Baku, Singapore (sprint weekend) follows, making it essential to build transfers and budget now. Players should consider rolling a transfer from Baku into Singapore for maximum flexibility, particularly if planning to deploy no negative or limitless at the sprint weekend. The Aston Martin reliability question will either resolve or force permanent avoidance — this is the last window where Alonso offers meaningful budget upside before his value projections drop off.
