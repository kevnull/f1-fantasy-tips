Race arc
Las Vegas is the first of the final three races, and the key strategic thread is the Mercedes ADO2 power unit introduction. If it debuts here successfully, it validates Mercedes' engine advantage in cold conditions heading into the final two rounds. The Piastri and Norris engine penalties this weekend could create a template for how grid-drop plays score under 2026 regulations — if they underperform expectations (like Hamilton's modest 26 pts from the back in Brazil), it recalibrates penalty-based captain strategies for the remaining rounds. Budget restructuring away from 3M trap assets toward Hadjar is urgent with only 2-3 races left; teams that haven't made this pivot risk bleeding points through DNFs. The Ferrari cold-weather vulnerability revealed in Brazil could persist through the season finale if conditions remain cool, making the McLaren vs Ferrari constructor decision a multi-race commitment.
