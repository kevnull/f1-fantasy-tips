Chip strategy
AutopilotUse
Strong consensus from all narrators. With 4A builds and high DNF risk at Interlagos (17 DNFs over last 4 seasons, rain forecast), autopilot automatically swaps your captain/DRS boost if one driver DNFs — massive swing protection. F1FantasyPolePosition explicitly says 'smoke them if you got them' for autopilot, final fix, or no negative this weekend.
No NegativeUse
If you've been saving this chip, Brazil is the payoff. 17 DNFs/DNS over last 4 seasons, wet conditions increase crash/retirement risk. F1FantasyPolePosition says 'your patience will be rewarded' if you held this chip for a rainy weekend.
Final FixUse
Wet weather could cause a tier-A driver to qualify out of position. Verstappen engine penalty rumors (unconfirmed) add uncertainty. Final fix lets you react after qualifying. Strong consensus to deploy defensive chips at Brazil.
WildcardSave
No narrator consensus to use wildcard here. Better saved for a cleaner race weekend where you can maximize a full team restructure without rain variance eating into returns.
LimitlessSave
Rain variance makes limitless risky — you want it on a predictable high-scoring weekend. BoxBoxF1Fantasy showed at Monza that 3x boost can outscore limitless in certain conditions.
3x CaptainWait
If you're confident in Verstappen dominating a wet race (as he did in Singapore and Malaysia), 3x could be enormous. But DNF risk at 12% and rain unpredictability make this a high-variance play. Consider pairing with autopilot for insurance.

Race arc
Brazil is the final race of a grueling triple-header and a pivotal moment for chip deployment. The rain threat makes this a defensive chip weekend — autopilot, no negative, and final fix all have strong use cases. After Brazil, the calendar moves to Las Vegas where conditions are dry and predictable, making it a much better candidate for aggressive chips like limitless or 3x captain. Mercedes look set to be the dominant constructor through the end of the season with Antonelli's form, and the Russell engine penalty now (just ICE, 5 places) means they likely avoid a full power unit swap and back-of-grid start at Las Vegas. Budget management is crucial: get inactive drivers (Ocon, Hirakawa) out immediately, lock in budget-rising B-tier assets like Bortoleto and Sainz, and position your team for a strong run through the final 3-4 races.
