Race arc
Austria is the start of a critical double-header with Silverstone immediately following. Budget building is paramount right now as several sources note the 4A-lineup (four A-tier drivers) is tantalizingly close for well-managed teams. Hulkenberg at the 3.0M price floor and Lindblad at 5.6M are the twin pillars of budget enablement — locking them in now sets up flexibility for Silverstone and beyond. The key strategic question over the next two races is whether Ferrari's engine update genuinely closes the gap to Mercedes or whether Mercedes reassert dominance at Silverstone, a track that has historically suited them. Managers who build budget efficiently through Austria while maintaining Antonelli 2x and Hamilton exposure will be best positioned for the second half of the season, where chip timing and team value will separate the top 1K from the rest.
