Race arc
Austin marks a critical inflection point in the season with only a handful of races remaining. Budget building enters its final phase — channels stress loading up on price-rise candidates like Hadjar, Lindblad, and Lawson now to maximize budget for the season-ending stretch through Mexico, Brazil, and Abu Dhabi. The Mercedes upgrade trajectory is the key storyline: if the older Baku aero specification proves competitive at COTA (as it did for Russell's Baku win), Mercedes remains the top constructor play through the Americas swing. If not, a pivot to Ferrari + Red Bull constructors may be needed for Mexico. Chip deployment becomes increasingly urgent — any unused 3x or Limitless chips should ideally be played within the next 2-3 races to avoid wasting them. Watch Verstappen's form closely; if he dominates Austin, he becomes the locked-in captain through the final rounds.
