Race arc
The Spanish GP at the Madring is a pivotal transitional race. It serves as the first real stress test of car reliability at a new, demanding circuit before the calendar shifts to Baku (Round 17), where George Russell is expected to take an engine penalty (making him a strong overtake-points play), Williams brings a season-defining upgrade (boosting Albon's ceiling), and the street circuit nature amplifies DNF risk further. Players should be positioning their tier B assets now for Baku — Perez and Albon are strong holds through both races. The grind phase of the season is here: most top players have used their high-value chips, so marginal gains from smart transfers and budget building matter more than ever. Aston Martin's reliability trajectory needs monitoring — if Honda PU issues persist at Madring, they become a hard avoid through Baku and Malaysia. Singapore sprint weekend (the last sprint) looms as a potential No Negative or chip deployment target if you save it past Madrid.
