Race arc
Las Vegas is a street circuit that rewards straight-line speed and energy management — Mercedes and Ferrari power units should excel. With Antonelli's WDC wrapped up, the focus shifts to the constructors' championship battle between Mercedes and McLaren/Ferrari. The rain variable could create chaos and favor veterans like Verstappen and Hulkenberg. Budget restructuring away from 3M trap assets (Perez, Stroll, Bottas) toward a leaner Antonelli + Verstappen + quality mid-tier core is the key strategic move heading into the final 2-3 races. If you haven't already pivoted to this structure, Las Vegas with its scoring volatility (rain, penalties, street circuit chaos) is the weekend to make the switch — either via transfers or Wildcard — to set up a strong finish through Abu Dhabi.
