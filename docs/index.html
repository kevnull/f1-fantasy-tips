Race arc
The United States GP kicks off a critical late-season triple-header. Ferrari's large upgrade package could redefine the constructor pecking order for the remaining six races — if it works, Ferrari become a long-term lock alongside Mercedes. The ML data for the Mexican GP (Round 21) already projects Mercedes as the top constructor and Ferrari very close behind, suggesting this Ferrari+Mercedes pairing could carry forward. Lawson's strong Singapore result will positively impact his 3-race moving average through Mexico and beyond, making him a better hold than Lindblad. Budget drivers like Hulkenberg are on track for continued price rises through the triple-header, so locking them in now is essential. Teams should position for Mexico (another high-altitude, power-sensitive track) while capitalizing on COTA's upgrade-driven uncertainty.
