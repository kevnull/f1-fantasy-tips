Race arc
Baku kicks off a brutal three-triple-header stretch to end the season (Azerbaijan → Malaysia/Bahrain → Singapore, then US → Mexico → Brazil, then Las Vegas → Qatar → Abu Dhabi). With most top players having used 5 of 6 chips, the remaining races become a grind where consistent team-building and budget management matter more than chip impact. Rolling a transfer from Madrid into Baku was a popular strategy (FanAmp specifically planned this via triple Mercedes). The Williams upgrade at Baku could reshape Albon's value for the remainder of the season. The Hadjar/Lawson situation at Red Bull is the key domino — if Hadjar returns, it creates a forced transfer for the ~30%+ of players running Lawson, potentially opening a budget gap. Budget is king now: players 2-3M behind their targets need to nail every price rise through this triple-header to stay competitive into the final stretch.
