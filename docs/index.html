Chip strategy
LimitlessWait
Consensus strongest chip option for Monaco. Ideal if your held assets (Colapinto, Bottas, Stroll, Bortoleto) are set for price rises and you can field a full-strength team without budget constraints. Many managers planning to use it here, especially those who held it through Canada. Individual-dependent — only viable if your non-Limitless roster won't lose too much budget while benched.
No NegativeSave
Monaco historically has only ~2.5 DNFs per race and very few incidents compared to street circuits like Canada. Rarely viable here. Only reconsider if rain is forecast closer to race day.
WildcardWait
Individual-dependent. If you need 4+ transfers to set up properly for Monaco, a Wildcard is justified. ReinFantasy used theirs pre-Canada to set up for Monaco with minimal transfers needed. If your team is already close, save it.
3X BoostSave
Not recommended at a non-sprint weekend where midfield scoring is extremely low. Better saved for a sprint weekend or a race where Antonelli's dominance is more certain (post-practice data needed). Multiple analysts recommend saving for Silverstone sprint or later.
AutopilotSave
Not viable without a 280-point driver asset threshold. Current meta focuses on constructor budget building.
Final FixWait
Cannot assess until after qualifying. At Monaco, qualifying position is everything — if a key driver has a penalty or crash in qualifying, Final Fix becomes extremely valuable.

Race arc
Monaco is a budget consolidation race where the primary goal is locking in 1.8M+ in price rises from Stroll, Colapinto, and Bottas while your premium picks (Antonelli x2, Mercedes, Ferrari) do the heavy lifting for points. The extremely low midfield scoring potential (5.26 avg over last 3 years) means tier B drivers are essentially dead weight for points — you're holding them purely for budget trajectory. After Monaco, the calendar opens up with more overtaking-friendly circuits where midfield drivers like Lawson (who may drop in price at Monaco) become excellent buy-low candidates. Managers playing Limitless at Monaco should be positioning their bench assets for price rises, then re-engaging with a stronger budget for the summer run of races. The key setup decision is whether to hold Lawson through a likely price drop at Monaco for his long-term upside, or swap to a safer budget builder like Bortoleto who needs fewer points to hold value.
