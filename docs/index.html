Race arc
Austria marks the second half of a double-header with Barcelona, meaning transfer economy is crucial — hold assets that work across both races (Lawson, Hadjar, Hulkenberg) rather than one-week rentals like Colapinto. The '4A light' meta is crystallizing around Antonelli x2 + Mercedes + Ferrari with Hadjar/Gasly as the bridge to full 4A builds (Antonelli + Leclerc/Hamilton as both A-tier drivers). In 3-4 weeks, top managers may have the budget for full 4A lineups, so every price rise matters now. The Red Bull Ring's sprint format adds scoring upside for consistent finishers, further rewarding reliability over raw pace — which is why Mercedes and Ferrari constructors remain the safest bets while McLaren's DNF rate makes them a liability. The Gasly/Monaco resolution could reshape B-tier strategy for weeks to come.
