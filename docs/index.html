Chip strategy
3x BoostUse
Highest-upside chip deployment of the season. Antonelli starting from back with 20-place penalty at his home GP. ML projects 45.5 exp pts with 90% CI up to 72 — with 3x that's a ceiling of ~216 pts from one driver. All channels unanimously recommend using 3x here if you still have it. BoxBoxF1Fantasy activated 3x on all teams except one. Historical precedent strongly supports grid-penalty captains producing massive scores.
AutopilotWait
FanAmp suggests pairing Autopilot with Antonelli as insurance — if Antonelli's race goes wrong (damage, traffic), the 2x boost automatically shifts to your next-best driver (Russell, Leclerc, Norris). Good hedge if you don't have the 3x chip. FanAmp notes it's a 'crapshoot chip' but the risk-reward profile is favorable this weekend.
LimitlessSave
BoxBoxF1Fantasy ran the numbers: Limitless scores ~372 pts vs 3x chip scoring ~380 pts on his budget. The 3x chip actually outperforms Limitless this week due to Antonelli's extreme upside. If you must use Limitless, go triple Mercedes + triple Ferrari + Norris, but 3x is the better play. Save Limitless for a sprint weekend or more balanced field.
WildcardSave
No channel recommends using Wildcard this week. Save for Baku where Williams upgrades arrive and Russell may take his engine penalty, creating a better restructuring opportunity.

Race arc
The Italian GP is the marquee 3x chip weekend of the 2026 season, driven entirely by Antonelli's engine penalty at his home race. After Monza, the calendar moves to Madrid (where Hadjar remains out and Lindblad could rise further) and then a week off before Baku — where Williams bring their season-defining upgrade, Russell is rumored to take his engine penalty, and Hadjar is expected to return. Managers should be thinking about rolling a transfer through Monza into Madrid if possible, and saving the Wildcard for the Baku restructuring window. Ferrari's ADUO engine upgrade could reshape the constructor meta if it closes the gap to Mercedes, making Ferrari assets increasingly attractive for the run into the Middle East races. Budget builders should target Alonso's easy price rise this week but be ready to pivot him out before Baku where Aston Martin's form may not hold on a street circuit.
