Chip strategy
Triple Captain (3x)Wait
ReinFantasy argues this is the optimal 3x weekend — second-to-last sprint of the season (Singapore is low-overtake and less ideal). Sprint weekends offer 60% more points on average. However, FanAmp suggests saving 3x for Monza if Antonelli takes an engine penalty (10-20 place grid drop = massive overtake points tripled). Consensus: use it now if you're confident in your captain pick, but Monza could be even better if Antonelli gets a 20-place penalty.
LimitlessUse
FanAmp explicitly recommends using Limitless this weekend if you still have it. Sprint weekend provides extra scoring session. Ideal Limitless team: Antonelli, Norris, Leclerc, Hamilton, Russell, Mercedes, Ferrari. Not the perfect Limitless weekend (low-overtake non-sprint would be ideal) but remaining calendar options are limited — Japan, Monaco, Hungary, Jeddah already passed. Sprint scoring makes this good enough.
No NegativeWait
Rain forecast for Friday and Saturday increases chaos and DNF potential. Aston Martin lead grid in retirements and have new PU. BoxBox specifically flags this as a good no-negative weekend. If you're running Alonso/Stroll, this chip provides valuable insurance against their reliability concerns.
WildcardSave
Multiple channels suggest saving Wildcard for when Hadjar returns (possibly Monza) as another asset reshuffle will be needed. Could also be valuable post-Monza when upgrade picture is clearer. Only use now if you absolutely need 3+ transfers to reach 4A and cannot absorb the point penalties.
AutopilotWait
BoxBox mentions Autopilot alongside No Negative as useful given rain forecast and unpredictability. Could protect against weather-induced chaos in sprint/qualifying.

Race arc
The Dutch GP marks the critical pivot point of the 2026 season. Hadjar's injury forces the mass migration to 4A lineups that the community has been building toward for weeks — this is the inflection point where budget-building ends and premium-driver stacking begins. Ferrari and McLaren's major upgrade packages will establish the pecking order for the European leg finale (Monza, Baku, Singapore). Antonelli's confirmed Monza engine penalty creates a strategic fork: those who save 3x captain here may be rewarded with enormous overtake points at his home race, while those who use it at Zandvoort capitalize on sprint weekend scoring multipliers. Aston Martin's trajectory is the sleeper storyline — if the Honda PU upgrade works, Stroll and Alonso become long-term budget machines through Baku. The key question entering Monza (Round 15) will be whether Ferrari's upgrades have genuinely closed the gap to Mercedes, or if this was a track-specific fluke at Zandvoort's slow-speed layout.
